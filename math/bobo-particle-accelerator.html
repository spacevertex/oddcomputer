Bobo Particle Accelerator

Deterministic particle motion simulator.

Launch the simulator →

Also try the 3D version, the isometric 3D view, or the 4D demo.

It has more in common with Conway's Life than it does with a gravity sim. Particles are only influenced by the closest particle to them at the beginning of each turn.

Two particle types

  • nearest: receives an acceleration of -1, 0, or 1 in each of the x and y directions each turn, depending on its closest neighbor.
  • bobo: does not move, only influences passing 'nearest' particles.
