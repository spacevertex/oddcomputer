Bobo Vector Automaton

Deterministic particle motion simulator.

Launch the 2D editor →

About the universe

  • Each tick, a particle accelerates by at most 1 on each axis, toward its closest neighbor
  • A particle is affected only by its closest neighbor; nothing farther away matters
  • Particles can occupy the same coordinates without colliding
  • There are only integers: each particle has attributes x, y, vx and vy
  • All interactions are resolved simultaneously
  • Tied closest neighbors vote on each axis and the majority wins; an even split means no acceleration on that axis

Two particle types

  • nearest: receives an acceleration of EXACTLY -1, 0, or 1 in each of the x and y directions each turn, depending ONLY on its closest neighbor.
  • bobo: does not move, only influences passing 'nearest' particles.

Similarities to Conway's Life

  • Deterministic, no rounding, no random interactions
  • Naturally occuring oscillators, gliders
  • Opportunities for finding and engineering more complex structures
  • Rules are very simple, behavior is complex

Differences from Conway's Life

  • Nothing is born or dies during run, population is constant
  • Rules are reversible, so previous conditions can be determined from current state
