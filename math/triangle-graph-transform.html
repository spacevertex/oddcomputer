Normal Triangle Graph Transform

Transform of some common graphs by calculating the area of a triangle for each point on the curve.

Launch the simulator →
  • For every point on the curve, construct a normal line through the curve, intersecting the x-axis.
  • Construct another line straight up or down to the x-axis.
  • Calculate the area of the triangle this defines, and use that value as y'.
