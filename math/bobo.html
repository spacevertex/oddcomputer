Placing a pattern (Load or Zoo)

ijkl
Move the pattern one step up, left, down, right. Hold Shift to move 10.
o
Rotate 90°.
u
Mirror.
;
Pre-roll: step the unplaced pattern on its own, one tick per press, to set its timing.
p
Preview: run as if the pattern were placed, against the live field. Press again to replay; moving or any other key ends it.
Enteror click
Place it. Esc cancels.

Adding one particle (+Nearest, +Bobo, marker)

ijkl
Move it, as above. It starts at the middle of the view.
Enteror click
Place it. A nearest takes two: first its position, then where its velocity points (shown as an offset).
p
Preview it against the field. A nearest runs at rest until its velocity is set.

Markers

m
Add a marker: a faint X for reference. It never moves, never counts as anyone's nearest, and is saved with the field.
,<
Show or hide markers.

View

h
Home: back to 0,0, keeping the zoom level.
3#
Next zoom level: Wide, Default, Close, then back to Wide. The view stays centred where it is.
drag
Pan the field.
click field
View menu: Wide, Default or Close, centred where you clicked.
click particle
Particle menu: Watch (nearest only), Zoom, Delete.

Trails

-_
Shorter trails.
=+
Longer trails: 10 to 5000 ticks. A dot on Trails means the length isn't 500.
