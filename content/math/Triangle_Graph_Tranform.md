# Normal Triangle Graph Transform
[Normal Triangle Graph Transform](https://oddcomputer.com/math/normal.html)
Transform of some common graphs by calcualting the area of a triangle for each point on curve.

For every point on the curve, construct a normal line through the curve, intersecting the x-axis.
Construct another line straight up or down to the x-axis.
Calulate the area of the defined triangular area, and use that value as y'
